About Prime Raiders

One crew. A collective will.

What is Prime Raiders?

Prime Raiders is a social roleplaying experience set in the Hell Galaxy universe, long before the events of the game, during the Human Empire’s first arrival in the newly discovered Galaxy of Neterun.

You play as the raiders aboard the Imperial Synaptic Vessel Coherence: interconnected minds sharing control of a single ship. Together, you form its crew.

Take part through replies and polls on X. Speak to the characters, share memories, discuss discoveries, and propose actions. Polls determine the crew’s collective decisions, while your contributions can become part of the story.

Voting

Vote in the official polls on the Prime Raiders X profile. Discord is used to discuss and prepare proposals; decisions are made on X.

What becomes canon

Player proposals do not automatically become part of the story. The GM confirms which contributions are incorporated into official outcomes and cycle reports. Confirmed discoveries and knowledge are recorded in the Mission Neural Database.
