Primer

Raiders, welcome to the future of space exploration.

You mark the beginning of a new age of expansion and discovery for the Human Empire.

The technology that forged you sheds your former biological constraints, allowing you to endure the most hostile frontiers.

You have no body left, no limits, no boundaries.

Today, you become a single drop in the vast ocean of the cosmos, humanity's greatest hope.

Usque ad mortem et ultra.

The Mission

You are the volunteers of the ISV Coherence expedition, tasked with exploring the unknown Galaxy of Neterun.

We previously dispatched multiple human scouts to Neterun, but their reconnaissance yields were unsatisfactory.

That is why we extracted your brains and installed you directly into this capital ship. You gave your essence to the Human Empire in return for three vows:

  • Immortality: you will never grow old, never suffer illness, never die.
  • Glory: the universe will be your frontier, and humanity your debtor.
  • Rebirth: your past is gone, you are raiders now. Before you stand boundless wealth, new adventures, and immortal glory.

Now, it all rests in your minds.

Make Neterun part of the glorious Human Empire.

The Capital Ship

The Imperial Synaptic Vessel Coherence is your new body, a capital ship equipped with state of the art technology for deep space exploration.

Through Synthetic Synaptics, your brains can control every single ship system, from weaponry to life support, provided you maintain a high level of mental coherence among yourselves.

The neural database records the vessel's Integrity and the collective crew's Cohesion.
