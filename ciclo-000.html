CYCLE 000 — AWAKENING

Vessel: ISV Coherence
Mission: Exploration of Neterun
Imperial Officer: Razan

ASSEMBLY COMPLETE
INITIATING CONSCIOUSNESS TRANSFER

We awoke in darkness, unable to feel limbs, breath, or bodies. Machinery hummed and metal creaked around us. A voice reached us from above, but we had no mouths with which to answer.

NEURAL ALIGNMENT DISCHARGE

A sudden pain passed through us. Our memories began to overlap, becoming difficult to recognize as our own. We became aware of one another and tried to make contact. Among the confused impressions, a shared memory surfaced: entrails and blood.

Something shifted around us, and we felt ourselves turning. Our first collective decision was unanimous.

COLLECTIVE DECISION CONFIRMED
Selected action: MOVE TOWARD THE VOICE

A blurred image formed in our minds. The voice introduced herself.

«Volunteers of the Coherence, this is Razan. High Imperial Officer. Leading this expedition into the newly discovered Galaxy of Neterun.
And mostly alone here, except for a bunch of pickled brains. Yours.»

We learned that our brains were linked to the Coherence and that our eyes were its cameras. Together, we could control the ship. Razan asked us to hold on to a memory we recognized as our own.

Her subsequent attempt to synchronize us with the neural database was interrupted.

SYNCHRONIZATION ERROR
UNREGISTERED CARGO: 1

«There's an anomaly aboard… Reconnecting the prosthetic synapses…»

Then everything went dark.
