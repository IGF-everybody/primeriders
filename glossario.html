Terms and concepts

No matching terms found.

Cohesion

The condition of the collective mind aboard the ISV Coherence. Its states are Stable, Damaged and Critical.

Cycle

A period of the expedition recorded in a ship report, starting with Cycle 000. A cycle normally covers one Earth week, but may last longer when the expedition requires it. Reports and mission logs use the same cycle number.

Extraction

A recently invented process that allows a brain to be removed from a body and connected to a starship's technological systems, preserving all vital functions and the extracted consciousness.

Galaxy of Neterun

One of the latest galaxies discovered by the scouts of the Human Empire, currently serving as the main focal point for space exploration.

Human Empire

The unified world government established following the heroic deeds of the pilot Redda, who ended the chaos of the first space exploration era to peacefully unite all of humanity for its expansion across the universe.

Imperial Synaptic Vessel Coherence

One of the capital ships dispatched to Neterun, central to the exploration mission of the galaxy bearing the same name. Its hybrid crew includes dozens upon dozens of volunteer raiders.

Integrity

The physical condition of the vessel. Its states are Stable, Damaged and Critical.

Raider

A bio-machine hybrid created when a brain, extracted from its biological body, is installed directly into a starship.

Re-Born

A human consciousness given a new existence through Extraction and integration into a vessel. The raiders share the ship as their new body.

Redda I

First Empress of humanity, who initiated the expansion of humanity into the cosmos.
